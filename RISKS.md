# Risks

> Track uncertain events that could affect safety, performance, schedule, cost, compliance, quality, or delivery. Escalate safety and regulatory concerns through the applicable organizational process.

## Rating Guidance

Define project-specific likelihood and impact scales before using numeric ratings. Until scales are agreed, use `TBD` rather than implying precision. Record residual risk after controls and identify an owner for every active item.

## Review Cadence

Review active risks at project checkpoints and when requirements, architecture, suppliers, operating context, or evidence materially change. Link risks to related requirements, decisions, open questions, and verification records.
